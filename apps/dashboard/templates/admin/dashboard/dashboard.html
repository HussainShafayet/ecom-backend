Revenue is the sum of paid payments (cash collected on delivery), bucketed by when each was paid. A later refund of a whole order drops back out of the figure it once counted toward. Refunded for returns is the money paid back on completed return requests, bucketed by when it was completed (not counted again for an order that was also marked refunded). Net revenue is revenue less that.
